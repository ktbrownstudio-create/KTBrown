About KT Brown

For the woman with intellect, gumption and style.

KT Brown is a designer clothing brand created for women who know who they are. Our pieces are designed with care, made to be worn with confidence, and built to stand out without trying too hard.

We believe what you wear should reflect how you think and how you move through the world: sharp, bold and entirely your own.

What we stand for

Intellect

Gumption

Pieces with character, made for women who go after what they want.

Style

Timeless shapes with a distinctive KT Brown point of view.
